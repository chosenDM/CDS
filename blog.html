Guide 01 · Digital marketing

Build a digital marketing strategy around your customer

Start with the customer problem you solve, not a list of platforms. Define one audience, one useful promise, and one action you want people to take. Then create helpful content that answers real questions, optimize your website pages for clear search intent, and use social media to distribute your ideas. Measure enquiries, qualified conversations, and sales—not only likes. For a Kenyan small business, a consistent Google Business Profile, mobile-friendly website, and fast WhatsApp follow-up can be a strong foundation.

Guide 02 · Online growth

Turn online visibility into qualified enquiries

Customers need to trust a business before they contact it. Show your location and services clearly, publish proof of your work, answer common questions, and make it easy to start a conversation. Add a direct WhatsApp call to action, keep your business details consistent across directories, and request reviews after delivering value. Review your traffic and enquiry sources monthly so you can invest more in the channels that actually help the business.

Guide 03 · Automation

Start small with business automation

Good automation begins with a repeatable process. Write down how enquiries, sales, stock updates, receipts, or follow-ups currently move through the business. Remove unnecessary steps, choose a single source of truth, and automate the most repetitive handoff first. Keep people involved where judgment is important, and check the results using simple measures such as response time, errors, and completed follow-ups.

Guide 04 · Operations

Use sales and inventory data to make better decisions

A growing business should know what sold, when it sold, which products need replenishment, and where money was spent. A connected POS and inventory workflow can reduce manual records, improve receipt accuracy, and make reports easier to review. Set a weekly review routine and use the information to improve purchasing, pricing, and customer service.

Guide 05 · Customer experience

Make feedback part of your service process

Feedback works best when customers can share it quickly and staff know what happens next. Ask for feedback at the right moment, categorize issues, assign ownership, and close the loop with the customer. Satisfied customers can be invited to share an honest public review, while negative feedback becomes an opportunity to improve the experience.

Guide 06 · Education

Make learning resources easier to find and use

Digital learning resources are most useful when they are organized by subject, level, topic, and purpose. Teachers can use structured notes and assessments for lesson preparation, while parents and students can use revision materials to identify areas that need more practice. A reliable resource platform should be simple to search and accessible on a phone.
