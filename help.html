Frequently Asked Questions

What is Chosen Business Manager (CBM)?
Chosen Business Manager (CBM) is an all-in-one business management system that helps businesses manage daily operations efficiently from a single platform. It includes sales management, inventory tracking, expense tracking, customer management, reports, and more.
How much does CBM cost?
CBM is available at KSh 300 per month. You also get a 1-month free trial to test all features before subscribing.
How do I sign up for CBM?
Visit cbm.chosendigital.co.ke, click "Create Account," and follow the onboarding steps. You'll get a 1-month free trial with full access to all features.
What services do you offer?
We offer Software Development, Digital Marketing, Graphic Design & Branding, Website Development, and Business Automation. Visit our Services page for full details.
How do I get a custom quote for digital services?
Simply contact us through our contact form or reach out via email at chosendigitalsolutions@gmail.com. We'll provide a tailored quotation based on your needs.
Is my data secure?
Yes. All our software products use secure cloud backup with role-based access control to ensure your business data is protected at all times.
Do you offer training?
Yes! We offer digital marketing training, software onboarding training, and business automation consultation. Contact us to schedule a session.
